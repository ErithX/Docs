
# ErithX

> ErithX is a clean, modern, and purposeful platform designed to help engineering students avoid unproductiveness and spend their weeks building real skills. Unlike traditional platforms that focus on vanity metrics, ErithX is an internal system-driven mentorship experience that synthesizes real coding and development progress to deliver honest, weekly performance reviews.

## The Problem with Traditional Platforms

Traditional preparation platforms focus on vanity metrics—rewarding students for solving 500 easy problems or maintaining artificial streaks. But volume does not equal progress. Students often get trapped in their comfort zones, padding their stats with trivial solves while ignoring actual engineering skills. 

ErithX exists to change this. We do not care if you have 2000+ ratings or 500 problems solved. We care about how you are actually progressing, if you are consistent, and if your work aligns with your actual career targets.

## The Three Core Pillars

### 1. ErithX Performance Analysis
The centerpiece of the platform. After a user connects their profiles (LeetCode, Codeforces, GitHub), our internal system carefully analyzes their activity, short-term goals, and recent trends. 

It goes beyond pulling stats—it synthesizes them to provide an honest analysis of what the user did across the week. It encourages progress but carefully analyzes unproductivity, providing a targeted roadmap and required topics for the following week.

**Target Alignment Analysis:**
The system judges user activity against their stated career targets:
- **Top Tier / FAANG:** High expectation for complex algorithmic constraints and system design.
- **Startup Level:** Focus on practical shipping, varied tech stacks, and core fundamentals.
- **Balanced:** A mix of competitive programming and engineering projects.
- **Custom Goal:** Tailored to specific user preferences.

**Engineering Quality Verification:**
We do not limit our analysis to platform metrics. We judge what projects users are building on GitHub. We evaluate if the project meets the user's target, if it is just a tutorial follow-along, or if it demonstrates real engineering.

### 2. Curated Contest Tracker
We track and arrange competitive programming contests from high-quality, relevant platforms. We do not just list down 100+ contests. 

- **1-Click Calendar Sync:** Users can add contests directly to their Google Calendar without needing to sign up or install browser extensions.
- **Email Alerts:** One-click sign up for performance analysis and email reminders before favorite contests begin.
- **Timezone Auto-Conversion:** Contest times are automatically translated to the user's local timezone.

### 3. High-Signal Resources & Career Insights
Currently in its early stages, this section focuses on the upcoming high-competitive era, making students aware of how to prepare smartly.
- **Vision Insights:** Articles like "How to use LeetCode tags that no one tells you" or "System Design actually needed for entry-level in 2026".
- **Silent Building:** Guidance on what to do beyond college—X networking, building in public, writing documentation, and more.

## System Tone & Accountability

ErithX is not a generic AI cheerleader that encourages everything, even mistakes. Our system tone varies based on user performance. If a user does not listen to mentor advice repeatedly, the system tone becomes stricter. 

Continued inactivity or repeated ignorance of targets will eventually lead to the termination of the user's mentorship access. This is intentionally designed not as a punishment, but to ensure students take their preparation seriously from their own point of view.

### Sample System Responses

**Example 1: Initial response when user behavior is neutral**
> "You have 247 problems solved on LeetCode with 119 Mediums, but only 14 Hard solves — which is under 6% of your problem volume. Your contest rating is sitting at 1335 and dropped slightly. That tells me you are comfortable recognizing familiar templates, but freezing on unseen algorithmic constraints. On GitHub, you have 25 public repositories including Field-EYE, FOOD-HUB, and Financial-Dashboard. Your Codeforces rating is 824 with a max of 965. Stop padding your numbers with trivial easy solves and push into Medium/Hard DP and Tree recursion this week."

**Example 2: Changing tone as time passes with user's unproductivity**
> "This is pathetic. You have ignored my targets for over two months now. Why are you even here?
> 
> Last week, I explicitly told you to solve 5 LeetCode Mediums and make 5 GitHub commits. Your response? Zero problems solved, zero commits made, and absolute silence. You didn't even log in once.
> 
> You claim to want to be a Balanced Generalist, but you are currently a specialist in doing nothing. Your active days this week are zero. Your annual GitHub contributions are zero. You are on the absolute verge of being suspended from this mentorship.
> 
> If you do not solve 5 LeetCode Mediums and make 5 GitHub commits by next week, do not bother coming back. This is your final warning."
